**Corrections and drops** (applied in the candidate):
- Tversky & Kahneman 1974 (doi:10.1126/science.185.4157.1124) exists, but the quote could not be verified (paywall). It is not quoted or used.
- No study was found that shows edited contours ending up closer to the auto-contour than independent contours are. Sources 8 and 9 show
  only reduced variability. Anchoring is therefore presented as a hypothesis the plan measures (A31.2, Job S), not as an established fact.
- Christodoulou et al. do not say that large test sets are needed for small differences. Only the CI-width finding is cited.
- The 2023 TotalSegmentator paper (doi:10.1148/ryai.230024) is not cited for the coronary model: that model is not among its 104 classes.
